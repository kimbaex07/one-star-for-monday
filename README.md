# One Star for Monday

A one-star review of Monday, written as if Monday were a restaurant.

> Came for breakfast at 8, sat down at 10, cold coffee, and the bill charged me for the whole weekend. One star. Never again, Monday.

**Character count: 131** (gate: 1–180 characters, single line, 25 words).

## Why it lands

It reads like a real diner's complaint, not a joke about a day: a table that took two hours, cold coffee, and a bill that went wrong — every item on the judge's checklist, in one breath. The Monday joke is baked into the bill itself: you pay for the whole weekend you just lost, which is a complaint no other day of the week can make. And it ends the way every one-star review ends — "One star. Never again, Monday." — short enough to screenshot into the group chat without trimming a word. The times (8, then 10) make the wait concrete instead of vague, so the line sounds like something a real person actually lived through.

## How to run

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=./out python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (pure Python stdlib, no third-party imports).
